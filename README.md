# Awesome Substack [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of the best Substacks, resources, and tools for readers, writers, and creators on Substack.

Substack has become one of the most important platforms for independent writing, journalism, and publishing. With over 35 million active readers and thousands of writers earning a living from their work, it represents a quiet revolution in how we consume and create media. This list aims to help you discover outstanding publications across every category, along with the tools and resources to get the most out of the platform.

## Substack Alternatives

If Substack isn't quite the right fit, here are the most popular alternatives for newsletter publishing.

- [Ghost](https://ghost.org/) - Open-source, self-hostable publishing platform with full ownership, built-in SEO, and custom design. Takes 0% of your revenue.
- [Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/) - Built by the Morning Brew team, with a built-in ad network, referral programs, and SEO tools. Flat monthly fee instead of percentage-based pricing.
- [Kit (formerly ConvertKit)](https://kit.com/) - Email marketing platform with automation, landing pages, and paid newsletter support for creators.
- [Buttondown](https://buttondown.email/) - Minimal, developer-friendly newsletter tool with Markdown support and clean aesthetics.
- [MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/) - Affordable all-in-one email marketing with automation, segmentation, and a drag-and-drop editor.
- [WordPress + MailPoet](https://www.mailpoet.com/) - Full ownership with newsletters sent directly from your WordPress site.
- [Medium](https://medium.com/) - Built-in audience exposure with the Partner Program for monetization, though less control over your subscriber list.
- [Revue (by Twitter/X)](https://www.getrevue.co/) - Minimalist newsletter platform integrated with Twitter (availability may vary).
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/) - Reward-based subscription model supporting multiple content formats beyond just writing.
